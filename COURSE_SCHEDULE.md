## Notes

- The schedule is now tight: every slot is used, and Ch. 13 is taught at a fast pace (4 classes for 6 classes' worth of material). If time allows, the first class to add back is a second class for Ch. 13 (separating vibrations from selection rules), followed by a review day before the final.
- Chapters 4, 9, 11, 12, and 13 now take more than one week each. To restore a "one chapter per week" rhythm, they could be split (e.g., 4a "Translational motion" and 4b "Rotations and vibrations"; 13a "Rotational and vibrational spectroscopy" and 13b "Electronic and magnetic resonance spectroscopy").
- With fewer lecture days (holidays), the first candidates to move to reading are the Ch. 4 visualization subsections, the Ch. 8 interpretations section, and the ESR part of Ch. 13.
- Possible future additions (perturbation theory, group theory) would need classes taken from elsewhere, most likely by moving more of Ch. 1–2 to reading.
