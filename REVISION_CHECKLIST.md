# Revision Checklist — Live Textbook of P-Chem 2

Prioritized list of improvements, from the November 2026 review. Tiers are ordered by value to students relative to effort. Effort: **S** = under an hour, **M** = a few hours, **L** = a day or more.

This file is not part of the bookdown build (it is not listed in `_bookdown.yml`).

**Chapter numbering note:** the Postulates chapter was removed and its content moved (collapse → Weirdness; fermions/bosons and Pauli → Atoms; time evolution, unitarity, and a superposition example → Operators; wave-function conditions → Models). Chapters after Spin are now one number lower than in the items below (e.g., "Ch. 10" below is now Ch. 9).

**Also added:** heteronuclear diatomics (HF, CO) in Ch. 11; polyatomic vibrations (normal modes, IR/Raman) in Ch. 13 (figures from `scripts/make_figures_spectro.py`).

**Progress (November 10, 2026):** Ch. 2 restructured (computed black-body, photoelectric, and hydrogen-spectrum figures; Bohr model section; worked numbers; summary table; retitled "The Failure of Classical Physics").  review questions aligned; chapters 10–13 (and 12b) rewritten in the style of chapters 4–5 with 12 new figures (generated by `scripts/make_figures_ch10-13.py`); borrowed passages rewritten; notation clashes fixed. Normalization of the particle in a box added (Ch. 4). Dirac notation intentionally kept out of the book: the few kets were removed.
